Appendix A. beta-1 AR (7BVQ) pilot (separate project; not part of the InhA result)

Setup: 7BVQ (beta-1 AR, carazolol-bound) with ND-2606-OPT complex (SMILES: CC(C)[NH2+]C[C@H](O)COc1cccc2c1c(=O)n(CCO)cc2; identity, origin and novelty unknown to the authors; no prior-art claim is made), TIP3P solvent only, 0.25 ns per replicate (251 frames).
Ligand RMSD (backbone fit): Replicate 1 mean 1.75 A (max 2.66 A); Replicate 2 mean 1.33 A (max 2.34 A).
Asp1138 contact (ligand N/O3 to Asp1138 carboxylate): mean minimum distance 4.14 A (Rep 1) and 4.28 A (Rep 2); frames with distance < 3.5 A: 0.0% in both runs; frames with distance <= 4.0 A: 27.1% (Rep 1) and 6.4% (Rep 2). The docking contact (2.82 to 2.85 A) was not maintained in either run.
Protein C-alpha RMSF: mean 0.97 A (Rep 1) and 1.06 A (Rep 2); Asp1138 0.64 A (Rep 1) and 0.49 A (Rep 2); pocket residues 1136-1140 show low flexibility.
Interpretation: The ionic contact did not form in either replicate, but the close-contact fraction differs between runs, so this result is not reproducible at 0.25 ns.
